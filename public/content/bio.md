Anjaneya Bhardwaj is an aspiring AI/ML Engineer currently pursuing a Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence at the University at Buffalo, SUNY. His work focuses on applying Agentic AI, Reinforcement Learning, and Deep Learning to solve complex, real-world problems across high-stakes domains such as healthcare, cybersecurity, and large-scale industrial procurement. His engineering experience spans the design of diverse systems: multi-modal classification models that fuse text and vision, generative diffusion architectures for medical time-series forecasting, multi-agent reinforcement learning systems for network defense, and orchestrated multi-agent LLM systems with parallel sub-agents and tool use. As Team Lead for an AI Engineering project with Globant X at the University at Buffalo, he architected an end-to-end procurement optimization platform for a Mexican fuel-station network of 617 stations with an annual procurement baseline of roughly 189 billion MXN (~$9.5B USD), coordinating a team of three and shipping a production-ready Streamlit dashboard with a bilingual conversational agent. Before his graduate studies, Anjaneya completed a B.Tech in Information Technology at Jaypee Institute of Information Technology in India, served as Admissions Head of the Light De Literacy NGO for four years, and led a startup initiative on early detection of lifestyle diseases. He is committed to leveraging his skills in PyTorch, LangChain, LangGraph, reinforcement learning, and modern LLM orchestration to develop impactful, data-driven solutions inside collaborative environments. He believes in legible models over clever ones, rigorous evaluation over benchmark theatrics, and that the most useful AI systems are the ones whose failure modes you can explain to a non-technical stakeholder in plain language.
